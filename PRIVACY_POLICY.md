# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [16/09/2026]

This Privacy Policy explains how VUONG DUC HUNG ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use **YouTube AI Learning Assistant** (the "Extension").

## 1. What the Extension does

The Extension helps you study a YouTube video. It can read the transcript of the video currently being studied, create quizzes and flashcards, assess your answers, and suggest timestamps to review.

The Extension is not a YouTube product and is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube, Google, or Google Gemini.

## 2. Information we process

Depending on the features you use, the Extension may process:

- YouTube video information, including the video ID, title, transcript text, language, duration, and transcript timestamps.
- Study-session information, including quiz or flashcard content, answers, scores, topic assessments, and review timestamps.
- Google account information made available through Google OAuth, such as an account identifier or basic profile information, only when you choose to sign in and only to the extent required by the selected Google permissions.
- Technical information needed to operate the Extension, such as extension state and error information.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information, passwords, payment information, or confidential material in a video transcript or study answer.

## 3. How information is used

We use the information above only to:

- obtain and display content from the YouTube video you choose;
- process transcript content locally for indexing and retrieval;
- generate quizzes, flashcards, and learning feedback through the configured AI service;
- calculate results and return you to relevant timestamps; and
- authenticate you when you deliberately use a sign-in feature, maintain security, and troubleshoot failures.

We do not use your information for targeted advertising, sell it, or use it to build an advertising profile.

## 4. Where information is stored and shared

The local RAG service runs on your computer. Transcript chunks, embeddings, and timestamps may be stored in a local cache on that computer. In the current version, the Extension is designed not to maintain a centralized account database or permanently store your study history.

When you request AI-generated content, relevant transcript context and the information required to generate the requested result may be sent to Google Gemini or another clearly identified AI provider configured by the Extension. That provider processes the information under its own terms and privacy policy. Review the provider's current documentation before using AI features.

Google OAuth tokens are used only for the authentication flow and are not sent to the local RAG service, stored in the local vector database, or written to application logs. We do not access or reuse Google AI Studio cookies.

We do not sell personal information. We disclose information only to service providers needed for the requested functionality, when required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users and the service.

## 5. Retention and deletion

Session data is intended to exist only for the active study session. Local cache data may remain on your computer until you clear it or uninstall the Extension and its local service. You can delete local cache data using the deletion or clear-data action provided with the local service, or by uninstalling the local service and removing its data directory.

If you signed in with Google, you can revoke the Extension's access from your Google Account security settings. To request deletion of information controlled by us, contact vuongduchung2004@gmail.com. Include enough detail for us to identify the request, but do not send passwords or access tokens.

## 6. Permissions

The Extension requests only permissions needed for the features you use. A permission may allow the Extension to interact with the current YouTube page, authenticate through Google, or communicate with the local service on your computer. The Extension does not use permissions to read unrelated browsing activity or to collect browsing history.

## 7. Third-party services

Your use of YouTube, Google OAuth, Google Gemini, and any other integrated service is also subject to that service's terms and privacy policy. We do not control the privacy practices, availability, or content of third-party services.

## 8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the Extension's design, including local processing where supported, restricted localhost communication, input validation, and avoiding credentials in logs. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

## 9. Children's privacy

The Extension is not directed to children under the minimum age required to use a Google Account in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Extension or applicable requirements change. We will publish the updated version at [learn.cndn.hungvd.com/PRIVACY_POLICY.html](https://learn.cndn.hungvd.com/PRIVACY_POLICY.html) and change the effective date above.
